fix(engine): silver badge — 5 bugs vs MtG rules

1. CR 702.136 — Plot "later turn" check missing. Engine allowed
   casting a plotted card on the same turn it was plotted.
   Fixed: plottedAtTurn tracked, castPermanent + legalCommands
   check state.turn.number > plottedAtTurn.

2. CR 702.16a — Protection damage prevention not applied in
   combat paths. markDamage in combat.js called without sourceId,
   so isDamagePreventedByProtection couldn't check source colors.
   Fixed: sourceId passed for attacker→blocker and blocker→attacker.

3. CR 702.16a — Protection blocking check was backwards.
   canBlock + declareBlockers checked BLOCKER's protection against
   attacker's colors. CR says "can't be blocked by [quality] creatures"
   — the check should be on the ATTACKER's protection vs blocker colors.
   Fixed: attackerProt vs blockerColors.

4. CR 702.16a — Protection damage prevention not applied in
   non-combat path. dealNonCombatDamage didn't check protection.
   Fixed: protection check added before filter prevention.

5. CR 702.16b — Protection detach check incorrectly skipped own
   attachments. The "doesn't remove your own auras/equipment"
   exception is specific to Benevolent Blessing, not a general rule.
   Fixed: removeIllegalAttachments now detaches ALL attachments
   of the protected color.
