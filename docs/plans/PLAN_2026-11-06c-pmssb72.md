# Plan B5: PMSSB-72 — batch 72 heuristic and shared-path audit

Data: 2026-11-06. Scope: only the three previously untuned generic paths listed
in `PLAN_2026-11-06b-batch72-rule-inventory.md`; no card-name scoring rules and
no full benchmark unless requested.

## Before probes and questions

1. **Creeping Peeper restricted mana.** Before B3, `spendOnly` recognized only
   the legacy Powerstone/artifact case; there was no generic purpose model for
   enchantment spells or face-up actions. Expected RED: the Peeper blue unit
   cannot satisfy the generic portion of Living History's cost or a morph
   turn-up cost; it must not be offered for an ordinary sorcery or activation.
   Check bot's offered command set and score, not only the mana helper.
2. **Living History attack trigger.** Before B3, the engine did not scan a
   controlled enchantment for a per-attacker `attacks_you_control` trigger and
   had no card-left-graveyard-this-turn fact. Expected RED: no queued pump after
   the qualifying attack; negative controls are no card departure, a token
   departure, and a reset at the next turn.
3. **Vortex Runner live threshold.** Before B3, static `cantBeBlocked` ignored
   the condition in the combat blocker legality path. Expected RED: a 2/2 can
   block at seven lands and cannot block at eight, while the bot sees the same
   live evasion in the attack projection. Probe the boundary at 7/8/9, not just
   the card's printed characteristics.

These probes test command/decision outputs and the path through the engine. The
older pre-B3 implementation has no batch-72 card descriptors, so the baseline
is the shared-path regression represented by the RED assertions above; the
pre/post table records those observable outcomes, not an unrelated win-rate
comparison.

## Shared-path audit

- Restricted mana: engine purpose predicate → source offer → pool accounting →
  generic/color payment solver → spell/activated/direct face-up payment →
  player view → bot purpose-aware spend model. Keep legacy `artifact` semantics;
  ensure a restricted unit cannot fund an ability activation, an ordinary
  sorcery, or a nonmatching spell. The `unlock_door` purpose is reserved in the
  model but no door card is added in this batch.
- Graveyard departure: all zone movements converge at `moveObjectDirectly`;
  count only card objects leaving their owner's graveyard, irrespective of
  destination; don't count tokens. Reset at turn boundary and include the fact
  in state fingerprints. Intervening-if is checked at trigger and resolution.
- Vortex: one `staticAbilityIsActive`/condition predicate feeds power/evasion,
  legal block validation, player view, and heuristic scoring. The threshold is
  read from the current battlefield, not frozen when the creature enters or
  attacks.

## Knobs and probes

No new bot parameter is planned. All three use existing, purpose-aware payment
and generic pump/evasion scoring. `tools/pmssb72-batch72-sonda.mjs` emits the
measurements; `test/audyt-pmssb72-batch72.test.js` pins useful relative
comparisons and fail-closed controls.

## Acceptance

- A qualifying Peeper source makes Living History castable and positively valued;
  the same mana cannot cast the ordinary sorcery.
- Bot chooses a useful Vortex attack at eight lands, not at seven when a legal
  blocker makes it a poor attack; the engine's blocker rule changes at exactly
  eight.
- After a card leaves the graveyard, Living History queues its attack trigger
  and the bot prefers the higher-value attacking target; without the fact there
  is no trigger.
- No unexplained scoring knob; report scores and focused test results in
  `docs/PMSSB.md` after the probes and any necessary adjustments.
